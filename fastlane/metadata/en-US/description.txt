Adopt a tiny pixel kitten, give it a name and make its little room a cozy home.

CARE AT YOUR OWN PACE
Feed, wash, play, cuddle and trim nails. As days go by, your kitten grows into a young cat and then a grown-up, each with its own personality. Your kitten has fullness, energy, happiness, cleanliness and health. Needs drift slowly while you are away, but your cat never runs away and never dies. No guilt, just cozy moments.

PLAY TOGETHER
• Laser Chase: guide the dot and watch your cat pounce.
• Yarn Ball: toss the ball and let your cat catch it.
• Toy Mouse: send your cat after a wind-up mouse.
• Garden Walk: stroll the path and dig up surprises.
• Go for walks to the park, the beach, the forest and the garden.

MAKE IT YOURS
Earn coins by caring and playing, then shop for food, toys, outfits and furniture. Decorate the room your way and dress your cat in bows and collars.

COLLECT MEMORIES
Take photos of your cat, fill your collection, earn badges and come back each day for a check-in reward.

LITTLE EXTRAS
• Home Screen widget shows how your cat is doing, and you can pet it right from there.
• Optional gentle reminders.
• Retro music and sound effects that follow your silent switch.
• Works on iPhone and iPad, with Dynamic Type and VoiceOver support.

PRIVATE AND OFFLINE
No account, no ads, no in-app purchases and no data collection. Your cat lives only on your device.
