Privacy Policy

Your cat lives only on your device. Meowchi has no accounts, no servers, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases. We do not collect, store or sell any personal information.

What stays on your device

Your kitten's name and progress, coins, decorations, photos you take in the game and your settings are saved only on your device. They are included in your normal iPhone or iPad backup and are deleted when you delete the app.

Reminders

If you turn on Settings → Gentle reminders and allow notifications, iOS shows local reminders that are scheduled on your device. Nothing is sent to us. You can turn them off at any time in the app or in iOS Settings.

Widget and sharing

The Home Screen widget reads a short summary of your cat from the app on the same device. When you share a photo, it is sent only to the app you choose; we receive nothing.

Apple

If you choose to share analytics with developers in iOS Settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. Apple processes this under its own privacy policy.

Children

Meowchi is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes and contact

Meowchi is made by an independent developer, shown as the seller on the App Store page. If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: mobile@othinks.com.
