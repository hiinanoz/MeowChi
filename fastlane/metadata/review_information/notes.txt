Thank you for reviewing Meowchi: My Pixel Cat.

Meowchi is a cozy, fully offline virtual-pet game. There is no account, no login, no server, no remote configuration, no ads and no in-app purchases. All content is bundled and works identically for everyone.

HOW TO REACH EVERY FEATURE
1. On first launch, tap "Meet your kitten", type a name and tap "Adopt".
2. The room is the home screen. The buttons under the room are Feed, Clean (bath, nail trim, litter), Play, Sleep/Wake and Health.
3. Play opens Cozy time, TV, a friend visit, the Laser Chase mini-game and Walks (park, beach, forest, garden). Activities use the cat's energy, which refills when the cat rests or sleeps.
4. The "Menu" button in the top bar opens Shop, Decorate, Tasks, Collection (photos and badges), Daily check-in and Settings.
5. Settings has music, sound effects and optional local reminders (local notifications only, asked for permission when turned on).
6. A Home Screen widget shows the cat's status.

PRIVACY
No data is collected. The privacy policy and support pages are linked on the App Store page.
